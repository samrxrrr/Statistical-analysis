STAGE 7 — STATISTICAL MODEL AUDIT
============================================================

PRIMARY QUESTION
Does a simple inferential logistic model add defensible information beyond the already-established dose-response trend and effect sizes?

MODEL SPECIFICATION
Continuous model: Muv_binary ~ TQ/50, binomial-logit.
Categorical model: Muv_binary ~ categorical TQ, reference = 0 uM.

CONTINUOUS MODEL
OR per 50 uM increase = 0.182944174 (95% CI 0.126554695, 0.264459339), p=1.65275167712e-19.

MODEL COMPARISON
Categorical-vs-continuous LRT: chi-square=0.087100744, df=1, p=0.767895832082.
This test evaluates whether the three dose levels require additional nonlinearity beyond a simple log-odds dose trend.

CRITICAL FORENSIC LIMITATIONS
1. The dataset contains no plate, batch, assay-run, replicate, cohort, or worm-ID structure suitable for cluster-robust inference.
2. Therefore the logistic model treats the 343 animal observations as independent conditional observations; this independence assumption cannot be verified from the available dataset.
3. The model is inferential/associational only and must not be described as a predictive classifier.
4. ROC/AUC analysis is not justified as a primary analysis and remains excluded.
5. No train/test split, cross-validation, or machine-learning interpretation is appropriate for this manuscript.
6. Because Muv_binary is deterministically derived from pseudovulva count, regression of Muv does not provide an independent mechanistic endpoint.

PROVISIONAL MANUSCRIPT DECISION: No evidence that the categorical model adds fit beyond the simple dose trend. Regression is therefore redundant with the primary Cochran-Armitage trend analysis and treatment-vs-control effect sizes. Recommended main-text approach: omit logistic regression.

FROZEN LANGUAGE GUARDRAILS
- Do not use 'predictive model', 'classifier', 'prediction', ROC, AUC, sensitivity, specificity, or confusion-matrix language.
- Do not interpret an odds ratio as evidence of pathway inhibition.
- Do not call the animal observations biological replicates.
- Do not infer toxicity, developmental safety, or molecular mechanism.
- If regression is omitted, retain Cochran-Armitage trend test as the primary concentration-ordering analysis.
