STAGE 9B — EXPERIMENTAL-UNIT / REPLICATION FORENSIC AUDIT
==============================================================

STATUS
------
PASS — DATA STRUCTURE AUDITED

DATASET
-------
N = 343
Groups = 0, 50, 100 uM
Source rows unique = True
Muv/pseudovulva dependency exact =
True

CORE FINDING
------------
The dataset contains 343 recorded animal-level observations.

It does not contain sufficient metadata to establish:
- number of biological replicate experiments
- number of independent cohorts
- plate-level clustering
- technical replicates
- batch structure
- animal identifiers
- assay/run identifiers
- observer structure
- randomization

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
---------------------
Repeated identical phenotype combinations are NOT evidence of
duplicate animals because source rows are unique.

However, unique source rows are NOT evidence of biological
independence.

STATISTICAL CONSEQUENCE
-----------------------
The existing analyses can describe and test the observed association
between TQ concentration and phenotype at the animal-observation
level.

They cannot reconstruct an experiment-level random-effects structure.

No unsupported mixed-effects or cluster correction should therefore
be invented.

REPLICATION LANGUAGE
--------------------
Use:

"343 animal-level observations"

Do NOT use:

"343 biological replicates"
"343 independent experiments"
"343 independent biological replicates"

UNRESOLVED LIMITATION
---------------------
Independent biological replication cannot be reconstructed from the
archived analytical dataset because plate, batch, cohort and replicate
identifiers are absent.

RANDOMIZATION
-------------
Source-row order cannot establish random allocation.

PSEUDOREPLICATION
-----------------
The risk cannot be fully quantified because clustering metadata are
absent.

The correct approach is transparent limitation reporting, not
statistical reconstruction of unavailable metadata.

RECOMMENDED MANUSCRIPT POSITION
-------------------------------
Present the study as a phenotypic, animal-level observational dataset
with a strong within-dataset concentration-associated reduction in Muv
occurrence.

Explicitly acknowledge that independent biological replication and
plate-level clustering cannot be evaluated from the archived dataset.

Do not claim that the large animal-level N compensates for absence of
biological replication.
