STAGE 9D — THRESHOLD ROBUSTNESS MATRIX
========================================

STATUS
------
PASS

DATA
----
N = 343
TQ groups = 0, 50, 100 uM
Thresholds = >=1, >=2, >=3, >=4 pseudovulvae

PURPOSE
-------
Determine whether the observed concentration-associated phenotype
reduction is robust to reasonable threshold definitions.

PRIMARY ENDPOINT
----------------
>=1 pseudovulva is identical to the recorded Muv endpoint.

>=2, >=3 and >=4 are exploratory sensitivity thresholds.

KEY PRINCIPLE
-------------
Threshold analyses are sensitivity analyses.

They do not redefine the primary endpoint.

ROBUSTNESS CRITERIA
-------------------
For each threshold we examined:

- exact prevalence confidence intervals
- Fisher exact pairwise inference
- Newcombe risk differences
- relative risks
- odds ratios
- Holm-adjusted pairwise P-values
- Bayesian posterior risk differences
- Bayesian ordered probabilities
- leave-one-out risk-difference sensitivity
- sparsity of positive events
- observed monotonicity

INTERPRETATION
--------------
The >=1 and >=2 thresholds are the most informative because they
contain substantial numbers of positive observations across groups.

The >=3 and >=4 thresholds become sparse and should not be used as
primary evidence for a concentration-response relationship.

The final manuscript should therefore emphasize the robustness of the
effect across >=1 and >=2 phenotype definitions while explicitly
acknowledging sparse higher-severity categories.

IMPORTANT
---------
No threshold analysis establishes direct Ras/MAPK inhibition.

No threshold analysis establishes absence of toxicity.

No threshold analysis establishes biological replication.

No threshold analysis establishes a continuous molecular
dose-response relationship.
