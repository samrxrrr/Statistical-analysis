STOCHASTIC DISTRIBUTION
-----------------------
Pairwise probability-of-superiority estimates are provided to
describe the full pseudovulva distribution.

The distributional analysis is complementary to the primary binary
endpoint and should not replace it.

BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
-------------------------
The key question is whether TQ changes:

1. phenotype occurrence; and/or
2. phenotype count conditional on occurrence.

The Stage 9A conditional analysis showed no detectable concentration
effect on pseudovulva count among Muv-positive animals.

Stage 9C therefore tests whether the whole-animal distributional
signal is driven primarily by the large shift in zero versus positive
phenotypes.

The final interpretation must be based on the actual threshold and
distribution results rather than assumed in advance.
