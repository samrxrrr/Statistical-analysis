This mathematical dependency explains why whole-animal pseudovulva

The conditional Muv-positive severity analysis is therefore the

OVERALL SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------
The authoritative dataset supports a strong and reproducible
concentration-associated reduction in the OCCURRENCE of the Muv
phenotype.

The data do not provide evidence that increasing TQ concentration
progressively reduces pseudovulva count among animals that remain
Muv-positive.

The revised manuscript should therefore describe two distinct
phenotypic dimensions:

1. Phenotype occurrence:
   strongly reduced with increasing applied TQ concentration.

2. Conditional severity:
   no detectable concentration-associated difference among
   Muv-positive animals.

This is a stronger and more defensible interpretation than combining
the two endpoints into a single severity claim.
