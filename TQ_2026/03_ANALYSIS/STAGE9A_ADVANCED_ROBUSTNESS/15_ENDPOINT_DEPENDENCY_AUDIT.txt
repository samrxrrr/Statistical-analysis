ENDPOINT DEPENDENCY AUDIT
=========================

N = 343
Muv == (Pseudovulva > 0): True

Interpretation:
Muv occurrence is mathematically determined by whether the
pseudovulva count is zero or greater than zero.

Therefore whole-animal pseudovulva analysis is a composite
endpoint containing both phenotype occurrence and the count
among affected animals.

Conditional analysis restricted to Muv-positive animals is
therefore the appropriate exploratory representation when the
question is severity conditional on phenotype occurrence.
