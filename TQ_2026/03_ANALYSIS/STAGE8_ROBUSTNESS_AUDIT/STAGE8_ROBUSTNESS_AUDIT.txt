IMPORTANT ANALYTICAL DISTINCTION

forced to agree.

severity value of zero when the scientific question is severity among
animals that actually exhibit the phenotype.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
The strongest and most defensible phenotype-level finding is a
concentration-associated reduction in Muv OCCURRENCE.

Among animals that remain Muv-positive, pseudovulva count does not show
a detectable concentration-associated difference.

Therefore the revised manuscript should NOT claim:
- progressive reduction in Muv severity,
- dose-dependent severity reduction,
- a severity plateau,
- direct Ras/MAPK inhibition,
- absence of toxicity,
- absence of developmental effects.

EXPERIMENTAL UNIT
-----------------
The analytical dataset contains 343 phenotype observations.

It does NOT contain identifiers establishing:
- biological replicate
- technical replicate
- plate
- assay/run
- cohort
- worm ID
- developmental-stage ID
- randomization
- blinding

Therefore n=343 must not be described as 343 independent biological
experiments.

FINAL DECISION
--------------
PASS.

Stage 8 robustness audit is frozen.

Manuscript statistical strategy:
- Primary endpoint: Muv occurrence.
- Primary inference: ordered concentration association.
- Effect sizes: risk difference, risk ratio, odds ratio with appropriate
  direction and confidence intervals.
- Secondary exploratory endpoint: pseudovulva count among Muv-positive
  animals.
- No ROC/AUC.
- No predictive classification.
- No unsupported logistic-regression prediction.
- No direct molecular Ras/MAPK inhibition claim.
- No unsupported toxicity/developmental safety claim.
- No unsupported plateau claim.
- No positive-control claim where no positive-control experiment exists.
